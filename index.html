Shopping Monster

Privacy Policy | Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Shopping Monster is a Chrome extension designed to help users understand clothing product materials, production origins and estimated brand markups.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension accesses, how it is used and how it is handled.

2. Information We Access

When a user activates Shopping Monster on a product webpage, the extension may access publicly displayed product information, including:

  • Product names and descriptions
  • Clothing material compositions
  • Product prices and currencies
  • Country of origin or production information

3. How We Use Information

Product information is used to analyse clothing materials, identify production origins where available, and estimate how much of a product's price may represent brand markup. The results are displayed to the user within the extension.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Shopping Monster processes product information locally within the user's browser. Product information accessed from webpages is not transmitted to external servers or third-party services by the extension.

Shopping Monster does not sell, share or disclose product information or personal information to third parties.

The extension does not operate a backend server or maintain a remote database for collecting user data.

5. Website Access and Permissions

Shopping Monster uses Chrome permissions to access webpage content and execute scripts needed for its product analysis functionality.

Access to webpage information is used for the extension's stated purpose and is not used for unrelated activities.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the extension. The extension does not request passwords, payment card details or account credentials from shopping websites.

7. Children's Privacy

Shopping Monster is not designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the extension or its data practices. Any updates will be published on this page.
